# Example Mutual Life — Medical Underwriting Manual (FICTIONAL)

This manual belongs to a fictional insurer created for the UW Lens demo. Ratings below are illustrative only and do not reflect any real company's guidelines.

## 1. Rating classes
Preferred Plus, Preferred, Standard Plus, Standard, then substandard Tables 2 through 8 (each table adds 25% to standard mortality, e.g. Table 4 = +100%). Decline or postpone where risk cannot be quantified.

## 2. Build (BMI)
BMI 18.5-29.9: no debit. BMI 30-34.9: +25 debits. BMI 35-39.9: +50 debits. BMI 40 or more: Table 4 minimum; refer to medical director if BMI 45 or more.

## 3. Type 2 diabetes mellitus
Evidence required: last two HbA1c results, medications, urine albumin (UACR), eGFR, eye exam, and attending physician statement (APS).
- Age at onset 40+, HbA1c below 7.0%, oral medicines only, no complications: Standard to Table 2.
- HbA1c 7.0% to 8.0%: Table 2 to Table 4.
- HbA1c above 8.0% or insulin with poor control: Table 4 to Table 6.
- HbA1c above 10%, or proteinuria with eGFR below 45, or proliferative retinopathy: Decline.
- Add +50 debits if current smoker. Credit -25 for documented HbA1c improvement over 12 months.

## 4. Hypertension
- Controlled on up to two medicines, average below 140/90, no target organ damage: Standard (Preferred possible if below 130/80).
- Average 140-159 / 90-99: +25 to +50 debits.
- Average 160/100 or more, or left ventricular hypertrophy: Table 2 to Table 4.
- Combined with diabetes: add ratings, minimum Table 2.

## 5. Obstructive sleep apnea
Evidence required: sleep study report (AHI, lowest SpO2) and CPAP compliance download (last 90 days).
- Mild (AHI below 15), or moderate with good CPAP compliance (4+ hours on 70% of nights): Standard.
- Moderate, not treated or poor compliance: +50 debits.
- Severe (AHI 30+) compliant on CPAP: +25 to +50 debits.
- Severe, untreated, or with lowest SpO2 below 80%, or with arrhythmia or heart failure: Table 4 to Decline.

## 6. Atrial fibrillation
- Single paroxysmal episode more than 1 year ago, normal echocardiogram: Standard.
- Paroxysmal or persistent on anticoagulation, CHA2DS2-VASc 0-1: +50 debits.
- Permanent AF or CHA2DS2-VASc 2+: Table 2 to Table 4. Postpone 6 months after ablation.

## 7. Japan-resident applicants
Recent tokutei kenshin (Specific Health Checkup) or employer checkup results may replace paramedical exams when dated within 12 months. Verify disclosure (kokuchi) answers against checkup abnormalities; contradictions require an APS.
